The Woodland Rebellion: tester notes

V1 test build · Acts I–II (Ch. 2–12)

Thanks for playing. It's an Oregon Trail-style journey with short 8-bit side-scrolling scenes in between. You travel, manage stamina and food, and get through a handful of chases, forages and fights. It plays on a phone or a computer.

▶ Play the game
This is an early test. All the art is placeholder, and the dialogue is a draft. Please judge how it plays, not how it looks or reads.

What we'd love you to try

  1. Play from the start ("New journey") as far as you can, ideally to an ending.
  2. Reach Chapter 12 and see both endings. Something is offered to you in the final fight, and you can take it or refuse it. The second ending needs chapter select or a replay, and it's easy to miss, so please try it.
  3. Watch your stamina. Tell us if you ever felt the game cheated you, or if you ran out of food and didn't understand why.
  4. On a phone: check whether the touch buttons feel OK and the text is readable.

On a phone, hold it upright for the trail and story, and sideways for the action scenes. The game will prompt you. On iPhone, sound won't play if the silent switch is on.

Chapter select

On the title screen, Chapter select jumps straight to any of the 11 chapters, with a typical party, food and berries for that point. Use it to retry a part or reach the ending quickly. It replaces your current save, and it asks you first.

Saves

The game saves automatically in your browser, and Continue picks up where you left off. Saves stay on that device and in that browser. They're lost in private browsing, or if you clear site data.

Saves may reset between test builds. If we change something that older saves can't handle, the title screen will tell you your save is from an older build. Then start a new journey, or use chapter select to get back to where you were.

Sending feedback

Use the Feedback button on the title screen, the game-over screen or an ending. It attaches the build number, where you are and your device, so you only need to say what happened. Useful things to tell us:

  • Anything that broke, froze, or looked wrong.
  • Anywhere you were stuck or didn't know what to do.
  • Anything too hard, too easy, or boring.
  • Which ending you got, and how it made you feel.

The build number is shown at the bottom of the title screen (for example v0.1.0 (abc1234)).
