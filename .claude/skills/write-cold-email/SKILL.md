# Three lines max

Short looks real. A long email looks like marketing.

## The three lines
1. **What they lose.** A specific, true cost to *them*, taken from the research hook. Quantify it
   where the source supports it.
   "Your firm's contact form says you reply within 2 working days. Most people who enquire about
   a claim have already called three other firms by then."
2. **What changes.** The outcome in their terms. It is **never** about the tool.
   "We make sure every new enquiry gets a proper answer within five minutes, day or night."
3. **A bold statement.** A confident, true closing line, not a question and not "let me know".
   "The firms that answer first get the matter."

Then the sign-off from `agency/facts.md`, and the opt-out line if the compliance rule requires one.
Neither counts toward the three lines.

## Rules
- ≤ 60 words in the body. One idea per line. No greeting beyond "Hi <first name>,".
- **Banned words:** AI, artificial intelligence, software, automation, automate, tool, platform,
  app, bot, agent, solution, leverage, streamline, revolutionise, cutting-edge, game-changer,
  "I hope this finds you well", "just checking in", "quick question".
- No links, attachments, images or prices in first-touch emails.
- Subject: 2–4 words, lowercase, specific to them (e.g. "new enquiries at <firm>").
  No clickbait, no "Re:" on a first touch.
- Every claim must be true and sourced from the research. If there's no real hook, don't write
  the email. Mark the prospect "no hook" instead.
- Write in South African / UK English unless `facts.md` says otherwise.

## Follow-up (one only, 4+ business days later)
Same thread, two lines max: one new, true angle on the loss, then a bold statement.
Never write "bumping this" or "did you see my last email".

## Self-check before saving a draft
- [ ] Line 1 is about their loss, not about us
- [ ] No banned words (scan for them)
- [ ] ≤ 60 words, three lines, a statement at the end
- [ ] Every fact traceable to the hook source
- [ ] Recipient not on the do-not-contact list, and not emailed in the last 90 days
